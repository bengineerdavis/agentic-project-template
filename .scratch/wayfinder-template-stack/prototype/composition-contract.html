PROTOTYPE — delete me. No persistence, no tests, no error handling. The question it settles: base publishes extension points + overlay declares requirements → is the mismatch detected?
Enforcement:

1 · Base — the noun

Meta-owned convention

base.provides:
  axes:              [is_distributed, has_entry_point,
                      gui, owns_service_lifecycle]
  extension_points:  [<path>, ...]
  bundles:           [<alint bundle>, ...]

overlay.requires:
  axes:              subset of provides.axes
  extension_points:  subset of provides.extension_points

The meta owns this schema. It does not own the bases' answers.

2 · Overlays — the adjectives

bound ships with its base — no contract needed.
portable must declare what it needs.
